PASS MEMORANDUM
Project Castellan / Castellan Diagnostics
Deal ID: KLD-2025-0097
2 September 2025
Prepared by Rowan Hale

CONSIDERATIONS
Castellan Diagnostics is a specialty pharma diagnostics business introduced by
Calder Peak Partners, the same sponsor as Project Meridian. Revenue is
USD 64,000,000 with Consolidated EBITDA of USD 11,200,000.

DOWNSIDES
The proposed structure carries an incurrence only covenant package. Opening
leverage of 6.80 to 1.00 is outside the mandate for the private credit fund II.
Reimbursement exposure to a single national payer is 58 percent of revenue.

DECISION
Passed. We declined on the covenant package and the payer concentration, not on
the sector. If the sponsor returns with a maintenance covenant and opening
leverage below 6.00 we would look again.
