INVESTMENT COMMITTEE MEMORANDUM
Project Meridian / Meridian Holdco II
Deal ID: KLD-2024-0118
28 February 2024
Prepared by Rowan Hale

RECOMMENDATION OF THE DEAL TEAM
The deal team pursued this opportunity and brings it to committee.

CONSIDERATIONS
Meridian Holdco II is a specialty pharma platform with a concentrated customer
base. Revenue is USD 128,000,000 with Consolidated EBITDA of USD 29,500,000.
The sponsor, Calder Peak Partners, is contributing 48 percent equity.

DOWNSIDES
Customer concentration: the top two customers are 41 percent of revenue.
The addback for run rate cost savings is 20 percent of EBITDA, which is at the
upper end of what we have accepted.

DECISION
Pursued. Approved by the committee on 28 February 2024.
