Project Meridian / Meridian Holdco II
Deal ID: KLD-2024-0118

PARTIES
Borrower: Meridian Holdco II
Sponsor: Calder Peak Partners
Administrative Agent: Northbank Trust Company
Counsel to the Lenders: Harrow & Dunne

SECTION 2. THE FACILITIES
2.1 Facility Type. A senior secured unitranche term loan facility.
2.2 Commitment. The aggregate commitment is USD 185,000,000.
2.3 Drawn at closing. USD 160,000,000.
2.4 Pricing. Interest accrues at SOFR plus 575 basis points.
2.5 Floor. SOFR shall not be deemed less than 1.00 percent per annum.
2.6 Maturity. The Termination Date is 14 March 2030.

SECTION 6. FINANCIAL COVENANTS
6.1 Maximum Total Net Leverage. The Borrower shall not permit Total Net Leverage
    as of the last day of any fiscal quarter to exceed 5.75 to 1.00.
6.2 Minimum Interest Coverage. The Borrower shall maintain Interest Coverage of
    not less than 2.00 to 1.00.
6.3 Definitions. "Consolidated EBITDA" means net income before interest, taxes,
    depreciation and amortisation, plus run rate cost savings not to exceed
    20 percent of Consolidated EBITDA for the relevant period.

SECTION 7. NEGATIVE COVENANTS
7.1 General Basket. USD 25,000,000.
7.2 Restricted Payments Basket. USD 15,000,000 in any fiscal year.
7.3 Investments Basket. USD 30,000,000.
7.4 Most Favoured Nation. 50 basis points, with a 12 month sunset.
7.5 Change of Control. A Change of Control is an Event of Default.

SECTION 8. MANDATORY PREPAYMENTS
8.1 Excess Cash Flow. 50 percent, stepping to 25 percent at Total Net Leverage
    below 4.00 to 1.00.
