AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO CREDIT AGREEMENT
Project Meridian / Meridian Holdco II
Deal ID: KLD-2024-0118
Dated 9 May 2025

The parties agree that the Credit Agreement dated 14 March 2024 is amended as follows.

1. Section 6.1 is amended so that the Maximum Total Net Leverage covenant level
   is 6.25 to 1.00 for the fiscal quarters ending 30 June 2025 and 30 September
   2025, reverting to 5.75 to 1.00 thereafter.

2. Section 2.4 is amended so that interest accrues at SOFR plus 625 basis points
   with effect from 1 July 2025.

3. Section 7.2 is amended so that the Restricted Payments Basket is reduced to
   USD 5,000,000 in any fiscal year.

All other terms remain in full force and effect.
